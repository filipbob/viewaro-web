FAQ

Good questions.

Does Viewaro include any channels or streams?+

No. Viewaro is a player — it contains no channels, streams or subscriptions of any kind. You connect your own playlist from your own provider, and Viewaro gives it a beautiful home.

Which platforms are supported?+

Viewaro is being built for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Android and Android TV.

What playlist formats can I use?+

M3U / M3U8 playlists and Xtream Codes accounts, plus manually added channels. EPG data is supported via XMLTV, either auto-discovered from your playlist or added manually.

Do I need an account?+

No — everything works in guest mode. An optional free account adds cloud sync, so your sources, favorites and settings follow you across devices.

When is it launching?+

Viewaro is currently in development and testing across all platforms. Launch details will appear here first.
